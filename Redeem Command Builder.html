Paste your keys, pick the bots, and copy the commands for the EasyRedeemer plugin. Everything runs in this page. Your keys are not sent anywhere and are not saved.

Your keys

Type an AppID once and it applies to every key after it, until you type a new AppID. The AppID can be on its own line or in front of a key (440 KEY). Keys before any AppID use the default AppID below, or ASF's own matching if that is empty. Lines starting with # are ignored.

The keys shown are made-up examples. Clear them and paste your own.
